Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions:
  • Camera not working:
    • Check that your webcam is properly connected
    • Ensure no other application is using the camera
    • On macOS, verify camera permissions in System Preferences
  • Model training fails:
    • Make sure you've collected images for all classes before training
    • Check that the dataset creation step completed successfully
  • Inference not detecting signs:
    • Ensure the model was trained successfully
    • Make sure your hand is clearly visible in the camera frame
